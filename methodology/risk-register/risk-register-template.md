## Severity Guidance

Severity = a function of Likelihood × Impact — use a consistent method
(e.g. a simple 3x3 grid: L/L and L/M = Low; M/M, H/L, L/H = Medium; H/M, M/H,
H/H = High) rather than ad hoc judgment, so risks are comparable across the
engagement.

## Status Values

- **Open** — identified, mitigation not yet complete.
- **Mitigating** — mitigation actively in progress.
- **Accepted** — sponsor has explicitly accepted the risk without further
  mitigation (record who and when in the Notes).
- **Closed** — risk no longer applies or mitigation is fully effective.

## Review Cadence

Review the full register at every phase transition, minimum. A risk that sits
untouched for multiple phases either needs action or needs an explicit
Accepted/Closed status — "still open" with no activity is a sign it's being
silently ignored.
